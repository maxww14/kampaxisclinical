Clinical technology with a programming mindset.

Kamp Axis Clinical is a specialized clinical data services company focused on EDC development, database programming, testing, and clinical data support.

Built around the needs of modern study teams.

Our goal is straightforward: help clinical teams turn protocol requirements into structured, testable, supportable electronic data systems.

We combine database thinking, programming discipline, clinical data workflows, and quality-focused delivery to provide practical support from study startup through database lock.

What we value

  • Clear requirements before configuration
  • Traceable programming decisions
  • Repeatable testing and QC
  • Transparent communication
  • Data integrity throughout the lifecycle

Our operating principles

Precision

Small configuration details can create large downstream consequences. We focus on getting the logic right.

Traceability

Requirements, changes, testing, and outcomes should be understandable and defensible.

Partnership

We work as an extension of sponsor, CRO, clinical operations, and data management teams.

Specialized EDC and clinical data solutions designed to help sponsors and CROs build, validate, and support high-quality clinical databases.
