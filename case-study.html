Demo study: oncology EDC build.

This illustrative case study demonstrates how Kamp Axis Clinical could structure a study engagement. It is a fictional example and not a client claim.

Study challenge

A hypothetical Phase II oncology study needed a structured EDC build covering demographics, medical history, concomitant medications, labs, adverse events, exposure, and scheduled assessments.

Approach

Requirements were translated into eCRFs, visit structures, edit checks, UAT scenarios, and a traceable test package.

Illustrative outcome

The demo workflow shows how programming and testing can be organized to reduce ambiguity and improve readiness for UAT.
